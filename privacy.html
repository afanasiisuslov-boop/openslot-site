Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026. Controller: OpenSlot, Paphos, Cyprus. Contact: afanasiisuslov@gmail.com.

What OpenSlot does

OpenSlot sends tour operators availability requests by WhatsApp, and calls them by phone if they do not answer. It records the answer so the seller can confirm the booking.

What we store

  • Operators: business name, contact name, phone number, preferred language, messages and call transcripts about requests.
  • Requests: tour, date, time, number of people, a tourist first name or reference given by the seller, and the operator's answer.
  • We do not store tourists' payment card data.

Why and on what basis

To confirm bookings the operator has agreed to receive requests for (consent and legitimate business interest). We message only operators who have agreed to be contacted.

Who processes the data

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages.
  • Vapi and a telephony provider place and record AI-assisted calls. The call begins by stating that the caller is an AI assistant.
  • Anthropic processes message and transcript text to read the operator's answer.
  • Hosting provider: GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

Requests, messages and transcripts are kept for 90 days and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop contacting you, by emailing afanasiisuslov@gmail.com. A reply "STOP" to any message also stops further messages. You may complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.
