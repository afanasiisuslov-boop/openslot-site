OpenSlot

OpenSlot confirms tour bookings with local operators on behalf of the seller (a travel agency or a hotel desk).

When a tourist asks for a tour, OpenSlot sends the operator a short WhatsApp message with the tour, date and number of people, and three answers: Yes, No, Other time. If the operator does not answer within 15 minutes, an AI assistant calls them by phone. The first sentence of the call says it is an AI assistant. The seller sees the answer on a board and sends the payment link only after a Yes.

OpenSlot contacts operators who have agreed to receive these messages.
